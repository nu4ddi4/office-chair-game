image data unchanged; curved dome projection and restrained color grading at runtime.
Near/mid/transition city, office envelope and facade shaders: project-generated.
Corporate Interior 05: project-generated carpet, static contact AO and accessories;
reuses the credited Poly Haven maple veneer, office stationery and chair assets.
High-resolution authoring master is separate from the 2K runtime export.
Interior 06 entry/server floor finishes and cleaned frame geometry: project-generated.

Interior 07: original project-generated laminate/fabric/metal roughness, fabric normals, carpet finish, shared desk props and network console laptop. Live equipment screens, office sample graphics and printed procedures are project-authored Canvas graphics. No new external asset sources.

Office Chair Chaos: the retained Interior 07 furniture and materials were rearranged into a 60x42m office. New shell, ragdoll characters, transport chair geometry, props, UI and procedural audio are project-authored. Original retained Office Chair attribution above remains applicable to desk-group decorations.
